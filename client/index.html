About Project

The Public Infrastructure Damage Reporting System enables citizens to report damaged roads, broken street lights, water leakage, garbage collection issues and bridge damage. The complaints are forwarded to the concerned government authority for timely action, helping improve public infrastructure and city services.

Government Initiative

This portal encourages citizens to report public infrastructure problems directly. Complaints are forwarded to the respective departments for faster maintenance and better public services.
